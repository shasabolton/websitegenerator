Electric Origami Hummingbird Plans - Paper Automata template

AUD$4.00

(These are digital plans only - for a physical version of this kit please check my other listings) With just a sheet of plastic transparency paper and some copper tape you can fold up this Electric Origami Flapping Hummingbird. No motors, magnets or coils are necessary as it operates by electrostatics. (Static Electricity). No glue is required as it folds up and locks together like a box. This designed takes its power from a common electric fly swat racquet which supplies the 2000V required to make it flap. The bird model can fit directly to the racquet or you can remove its circuit board and fold up the origami base to put it in. Upon purchasing, you will receive a PDF file with plans which can be printed on a home printer. This provides the template and graphics for you to cut out. Other materials you will need are - 0.1mm A4 plastic transparency paper to print onto (office supplies store) - 6mm wide copper tape (1 meter) (music or lead lighting store) - One electric fly swat with a diamond shaped mesh (bargain store) - 20mm wide copper tape (20cm) (optional) All of the above parts can be found on ebay. tools required - ink jet printer - fine sharp scissors - craft knife You will receive video instructions to follow along with while building the model. The fly swat has high voltage but low current. It will not cause any harm but can hurt like an electric fence if you are shocked, so take care to follow the instructions, remove batteries and discharge the capacitor before touching the copper terminals. If you have a pace maker or a heart condition you may want to avoid this project or take extra care.
