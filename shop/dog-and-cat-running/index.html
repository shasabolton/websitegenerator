Dog and Cat Running

AUD$49.00

This automata kit contains all parts and instructions to build two separate hand cranked moving models. Both a running cat and a running dog. It is a simple but interesting mechanism to watch as the animals leap and bound on your table. Parts generally can be pressed together. Any loose parts can be glued.
