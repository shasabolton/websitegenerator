Street Sweeper

AUD$140.00

A wooden toddler puppy car that actually sweeps up the floor as they go. I have had heaps of fun watching my little two year old learn to use him. I sprinkle some rice or marbles on the floor and she loves watching it disappear and reappear inside Pug Chugs tummy when she lifts the lid to peer inside. Caster wheels at the back allow for steering and rotating brushes at the front to sweep things into the catchment box. Pug Chug is available fully assembled, or in kit form for those who enjoy a craft project. Assembly takes around one hour with the detailed visual instructions provided.
