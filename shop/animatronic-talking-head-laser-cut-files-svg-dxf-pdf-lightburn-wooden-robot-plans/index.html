Animatronic Talking Head Laser Cut Files, SVG DXF, PDF, Lightburn, Wooden Robot Plans

AUD$16.00

Build an animatronic that doesn't just talk. It talks back. These are digital plans for a wooden talking head. You laser-cut the face, then fit three small servos and an esp32 controller. Put the phone you already own behind the forehead, open our web app, and have a conversation with it. It only listens when you hold the on-screen button or lean in close. Your phone sits behind the character's forehead. Its speaker is the voice, its microphone is the ear, and its camera notices when you lean in. The app sends the mouth and face movements to the robot over Bluetooth. The mouth syncs with the audio and the eyes track the motion of your face from side to side. The character can speak prerecorded audio files as well as live AI-generated responses to your questions. This download is the plans and the software, not a finished kit. You will get: - SVG and DXF files to laser-cut the wooden mechanism (all parts fit within a 40cmx30cm sheet) - PDF assembly instructions with diagrams - Video assembly and usage instructions: https://www.contraptioncart.com/talking-head-help/ - The Talking Head web app, for voice and conversation - ESP32 firmware to comunicate with the web app. You will need: - Wood: 3mm (1/8th inch) thick plywood or mdf (files work for 2.8mm - 3.2mm without modifying. Apply your kerf offsets for snug fits and best results with the mechanism) - A phone. Android with Chrome works directly. iPhone needs the free Bluefy browser, because Safari cannot connect. A computer works in Chrome or Edge. - Three 9g micro servos (180°), with the standard arms and screws - An ESP32-S3 development board with 16 MB of flash. You can purchase our custom controller board which includes servo power distribution, so the servos plug straight in. Your own ESP32 also works: wire servo power yourself, use pins 1, 2, and 3, and flash our firmware from a computer over USB inside the firmware section of our web app. - 4 extra servo mounting screws for the controller - Your own 5V USB charger, 2–3A. This powers the controler and the servos. The phone does not. PVA or super glue A small Phillips screwdriver Finished size: 22 cm × 17 cm × 5 cm You may use a board of your choice to move the three servos but if you want our character voices, AI conversation, face tracking etc, you will need an ESP32 running our firmware, connected over Bluetooth to our web app. How a conversation works Open the web app, pair the board, and start talking. Use a preset character, or make your own. There are three ways to give a custom character a voice: Recorded. Upload an MP3 and the face performs it. Scripted. Type a line and the app speaks it in a voice you choose. Save that audio, or let the app generate it each time. Original. Write a short personality The character uses ai to come up with replies and answers you in your chosen voice. Each reply is new. Playing your own audio, or a clip you have already saved, is free. Generating speech and live conversation are pay as you go, with no subscription. Credit starts at AUD$2, which should give around 30 minutes of solid, no-pause conversation. For you to ask a question and have a reply, expect to use up about 1 cent Australian of credit. Using AI image prompts will use up credit more quickly. Unused credit lasts 7 days, and using it starts that timer again. If you use the AI to create permanent audio files, credit is no longer required to play them after they are generated.
